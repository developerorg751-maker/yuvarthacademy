GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries regarding our programs and admission process.

Which classes does Yuvarth Academy teach?
Yuvarth Academy offers coaching for students from Class 6th to Class 12th, covering Pre-Foundation and Foundation levels.
Which boards are covered?
We cover CBSE, ICSE, and UP Board curricula comprehensively with tailored study schedules and board-pattern examinations.
Does Yuvarth Academy prepare students for JEE and NEET?
Yes! We provide structured competitive exam preparation for both JEE (Main & Advanced) and NEET UG, focusing on deep conceptual clarity and problem-solving techniques.
What competitive exams are covered?
Our competitive coverage includes JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET UG, CUET, and Science/Math Olympiads.
How can I enquire about admission?
You can fill out the Admission Enquiry form above to connect directly on WhatsApp, or call us directly at 9307023363, or visit our centre at Azad Chowk, Gorakhpur.
Where is Yuvarth Academy located?
We are located at Azad Chowk, Rustampur, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh (Near Post Office).
